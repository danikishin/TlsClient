# Code of Conduct

TlsClient is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include being constructive, assuming good intent,
accepting feedback, respecting different experience levels, and focusing technical
discussion on evidence. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized content, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing,
or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and
other contributions that violate these standards. Temporary or permanent participation
restrictions may be applied proportionately. Reports should be sent privately through
the repository owner's GitHub profile contact rather than discussed publicly. All
reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public spaces. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a
violation.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html),
version 2.1.
